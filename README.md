# Irons Pub Bingo

Play your clan's bingo event with a board right in RuneLite. The plugin tracks your tiles
as you play and adds up progress across your team: drops, boss kill counts, pets, XP,
agility laps, loot value and chat messages.

## Getting started

1. Open the bingo icon in the sidebar.
2. In the plugin settings, turn on **Use team store** and paste the URL from your host.
3. In the panel, press **Import board from store**, then **Choose team**.
4. Play. Tiles turn amber on progress and green when complete.

The **Get started** card in the panel always shows the next step, and the **?** button
opens the in-game help.

## Good to know

- Click a tile to see its goals and who did what. Right-click it to pin it to the screen.
- Missed progress? Open the tile's **Actions** and press **Request admin credit**.
- Keep RuneLite's **Loot Tracker** plugin on. Drops come from it.
- XP and kill count goals count from the event start, or from when you import the board
  if that's later.

## Privacy

The team store and Discord posts are off by default. When you turn them on, the plugin
sends your in-game name, your bingo progress and a hashed account id to the store URL
your host gave you, and screenshots to your own Discord webhook. These servers are run by
your host and Discord, not by RuneLite, and they see your IP address.
